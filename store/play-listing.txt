FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Built for oil and gas contractors who live hitch to hitch: log the day in seconds, and know exactly what you're owed, where you worked, and when your certs expire.

THE DAY, LOGGED IN SECONDS
Day rate, per-diem, mileage — tap, done, back to work. HitchWell keeps the running totals for the hitch, the month, and the year.

MULTI-STATE, HANDLED
Working three states in one rotation? HitchWell tracks where every day happened, so tax season doesn't start with an archaeology project of old texts and crew-change memories.

CERTS THAT NEVER AMBUSH YOU
Safety cards, medicals, endorsements — with expiration dates that warn you before the gate does.

YOUR NUMBERS, YOURS
Everything stays on your phone, lockable behind your fingerprint. No account, no cloud, nobody's dashboard.

You do the hitch. HitchWell does the arithmetic.
